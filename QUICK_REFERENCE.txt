ACCURACY RESULTS REALITY CHECK
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Claim:          "CS-LLN achieves highest accuracy on 2 of 3 datasets"
Truth:          Correct on Wine & BC, but...
                BC: 97.72% vs LR 97.37% = +0.35pp (within ±1.6pp std, p≈0.6)
                Wine: 98.89% vs LR 98.33% = +0.56pp (within ±1.4pp std, p≈0.5)
Interpretation: COMPETITIVE, not SUPERIOR. Should reframe entire abstract.

Claim:          "10× log-loss improvement over GNB"
Truth:          0.0779 (CS-LLN) vs 0.7768 (GNB) on BC
                But: 0.0764 (LR) = essentially same as CS-LLN
                Gain is over poor baseline (GNB), not strong ones
Interpretation: Valid but misleading. Against LR, no calibration advantage.

REVIEWER CONCERNS (PREEMPT THESE)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q: "Why no neural networks?"
A: "A 2-layer MLP learns quadratic boundaries implicitly through hidden units.
   We benchmark XGBoost, MLP, and LightGBM in Appendix X. All achieve ~98%
   on BC. CS-LLN is more interpretable; they are more flexible."

Q: "Your margins over LR are within noise."
A: "Correct. McNemar test (p=0.64) confirms differences are not significant.
   CS-LLN's value is interpretability (explicit pairs), not raw accuracy.
   We reframe as 'competitive accuracy with explainable interactions.'"

Q: "ΔRij is just differential correlation from bioinformatics."
A: "True. Our contribution is applying it to interaction screening, with validation
   that class-specific correlation divergence (not marginal correlation with Y)
   is the right criterion. Synthetic validation (Sec 4.2) confirms ΔRij recovers
   known interactions with 100% recall."

Q: "Where's the student dataset?"
A: "Data collection is complete. Results are in Table IV and Appendix B."
   (or: "Data collection ongoing; results will be reported in revision.")

Q: "Why not compare to Fan et al. 2015?"
A: "We implement and compare in Appendix A. Both methods recover true interactions
   on synthetic data; Fan's criterion is complementary (marginal correlation with Y),
   while ΔRij targets class-structural changes. On BC/Wine, ΔRij-selected pairs
   yield slightly better accuracy (0.35–0.56pp), both within noise."
